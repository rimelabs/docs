Coda and Mist v3 are the two models that outlive the retirements. A voice name is not carried by every model, so changing `modelId` while keeping the same `speaker` can leave you naming a voice the target does not serve. Check your voice here first.

<Warning>**If your voice is not in the table below, neither Coda nor Mist v3 serves it, and you need to choose a different voice rather than change `modelId`.** That applies to 270 of the 523 names in the catalog, including 204 of Arcana's 266. A shared name is also no guarantee of identical audio: each model is trained separately, so treat a match as permission to try the switch and compare audio on your own text before moving production traffic.</Warning>

## What survives from the models being retired

| Currently on | Why you are moving | Voices | Also on Coda | Also on Mist v3 | On neither |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Arcana | retires August 15, 2026 | 266 | 62 | 8 | 204 |
| Mist v2 | superseded by Mist v3 | 138 | 7 | 71 | 63 |
| Mist | superseded by Mist v3 | 117 | 3 | 51 | 64 |

Arcana is the hardest move: 204 of its 266 voices have no counterpart on either surviving model, so most Arcana users need a voice change rather than a parameter change. Only 9 names are on both Coda and Mist v3, so switching between the two surviving models is not a drop-in change either.
