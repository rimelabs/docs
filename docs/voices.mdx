Set `speaker` on every TTS request. For a first Coda request, try `astra`; use the live catalog endpoints for the complete, current lineup.

```json
{
  "speaker": "astra",
  "text": "Hello from Rime!",
  "modelId": "coda",
  "lang": "en"
}
```

Already using a voice and moving off a retiring model? A voice name is not carried by every model: 204 of Arcana's 266 voices have no Coda counterpart. Search the [Coda](/docs/voices-coda) or [Mist v3](/docs/voices-mist-v3) catalog for your voice before you change `modelId`.

## Languages

Language support varies by model. As of August 2026, Rime's current models support the following languages:

| Language | ISO codes | Coda | Mist v3 |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| English | `eng` / `en` | ✅ | ✅ |
| Arabic | `ara` / `ar` | ✅ | ❌ |
| French | `fra` / `fr` | ✅ | ✅ |
| German | `ger` / `de` | ✅ | ✅ |
| Hindi | `hin` / `hi` | ✅ | ❌ |
| Japanese | `jpn` / `ja` | ✅ | ❌ |
| Portuguese | `por` / `pt` | ✅ | ❌ |
| Spanish | `spa` / `es` | ✅ | ✅ |

Deprecated models keep their existing coverage until retirement. Mist v2 supports English, French, German, and Spanish. Arcana serves its legacy lineup (English, Arabic, French, German, Hebrew, Hindi, Japanese, Portuguese, and Spanish) until the August 15, 2026 sunset.

<Note>**A voice serves one language, so picking a language narrows your choices.** Coda covers eight languages, but no individual Coda voice crosses between them: 121 of its 184 voices are English, then Spanish 25, Japanese 10, Portuguese 8, German 7, Arabic 6, French 5, and Hindi 2. Set `lang` to the language of the voice you chose. An unsupported pairing may not return an error, so verify against the [Coda catalog](/docs/voices-coda) rather than assuming a familiar voice speaks your language.</Note>

<Warning>**Hebrew and Sinhala have no Coda equivalent.** [Coda](/docs/models#coda) does not serve Hebrew, so Hebrew and Sinhala both remain on Arcana after August 15, 2026. Every other Arcana language is served by Coda. If you use Arcana Hebrew (`aviva`, `ori`), contact [support@rime.ai](mailto:support@rime.ai) before migrating.</Warning>

## Arcana v3 voices

<Tip>Choose Coda for new projects and existing Arcana traffic. It scores higher in Rime's human evaluations for naturalness, prosody, and artifact-free output. See [Start with Coda](/docs/introduction#start-with-coda).</Tip>

Arcana v3 is Rime's previous flagship: a multilingual model with 266 voices spanning age groups, regional accents, cultural backgrounds, and energy profiles.

See the [full voice list](/api-reference/data/voices) and [voice details](/api-reference/data/voice-details) for available Arcana speakers.

### Arcana flagship voices

64 of Arcana's 266 voices are marked `flagship` in the catalog, across English (US regional, international) plus the other [supported Arcana languages](#languages). See [`/data/voices/voice_details.json`](/api-reference/data/voice-details) for the full catalog with demographics; filter on `"flagship": true`.

## Mist v3 voices

Mist v3 carries 78 voices with significantly lower TTFA than Mist v2. Most are drawn from the Mist v2 lineup, but the lineups are not identical: 71 speakers are shared, 67 Mist v2 speakers are not available on Mist v3, and 7 are Mist v3 only. Confirm a speaker before switching `modelId`.

See the [full voice list](/api-reference/data/voices) for available Mist v3 speakers.

## Mist v2 voices

Mist v2 is tuned for clarity, pronunciation accuracy, and high-throughput production. For new projects, prefer Mist v3.

See the [full voice list](/api-reference/data/voices) for available Mist v2 speakers.
