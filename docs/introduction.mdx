Rime turns written text into natural-sounding speech.

You provide the words and choose a voice. Rime returns audio that your application can play, save, or stream as it is generated. You do not need previous experience with speech synthesis or audio engineering to get started.

## What can you build with Rime?

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Add a voice to an application" icon="wave-sine">
    Turn messages, articles, lessons, notifications, or any other text into speech.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Create a conversational voice agent" icon="microphone-lines">
    Give an AI assistant a voice so it can respond naturally during a conversation.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Generate and stream audio" icon="bolt">
    Create complete audio files or start playing speech while the rest is still being generated.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## How Rime works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Provide the words">
    Start with any text you want your listener to hear.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose how it should sound">
    Select a **voice** for the speaker and a **model** for the speech-generation behavior. The quickstart gives you working defaults, so you do not need to make these decisions immediately.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive the audio">
    Rime generates speech that your application can save, play, or stream to a listener.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A few terms you will see

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Text-to-speech (TTS)" icon="comment-audio">

  <Card title="Voice" icon="headphones">
    The individual speaker your listeners hear.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Model" icon="layer-group">
    The speech engine that determines capabilities such as language support and control.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Streaming" icon="signal-stream">
    Playing the beginning of the audio before the complete response has finished generating.
  </Card>
</Columns>
